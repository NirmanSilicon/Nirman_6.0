IS 2745:1983: Specification for Non-Metal Helmet for Firemen and Civil Defence Personnel

Applicable Product / Application: Head protection for fire fighting personnel, civil defence forces, and emergency rescue workers.

Scope & Specification Domain

Covers requirements regarding material, design, construction, finish, and performance for non-metal helmets intended for fire fighting, rescue, and civil defence operations.

AI-Generated Explanation (Grounded Evidence)
Grounded Evidence

IS 2745:1983 (Specification for Non-Metal Helmet for Firemen and Civil Defence Personnel) is identified as an allied Indian Standard in emergency services personal protection. It covers high-temperature thermal resistance, flame retardance, and impact protection for civil defence and rescue personnel, distinct from routine industrial workplace headgear.

Supporting Evidence Factors:
  • Personal protective emergency headgear alignment in BIS catalogue
  • Thermal radiant, flame, and ember resistance requirements
  • Quality Control Order S.O. 4649(E) regulatory coverage
TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
  • Shell: Heat-resistant, non-metallic composite shell providing thermal barrier and projectile deflection.
  • Headband and cradle assembly: Flame-retardant harness providing structural standoff clearance from shell crown.
  • Neck curtain / flap attachment: Protection against falling embers, sparks, and hot liquids.
  • Chin strap with quick-release fastener: Secure retention under operational emergency conditions.
  • Reflective markings: High-visibility retro-reflective finish for low-light rescue operations.
  • Workmanship and finish: Smooth non-conductive surfaces free from hazardous burrs or defects.
  • Mass: Controlled mass ensuring operational mobility during prolonged emergency response.
PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
  • Shock absorption resistance under elevated thermal radiant conditioning
  • Penetration resistance under thermal and ambient exposure
  • Flammability resistance (self-extinguishing after direct Bunsen burner flame exposure)
  • Electrical insulation resistance (high-voltage protection against fallen power lines)
  • Radiant heat resistance (mitigation of heat transfer to wearer skull)
  • Water absorption resistance
SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
  • Thermal barrier protection against radiant heat and burning embers in firefighting
  • Mechanical impact and falling masonry hazard protection during structural rescue
  • Electrical shock protection from accidental contact with exposed live overhead wiring
  • Molten debris deflection without thermoplastic melting onto wearer head
  • Secure retention during energetic rescue maneuvers
TESTING REQUIREMENTS (IS 2745)
  • Clause 6: Design and construction – Inspection of non-metallic shell, standoff clearance, and neck curtain mountings.
  • Clause 7.1: Shock absorption test – Drop striker impact test onto headform measuring peak transmitted force after thermal radiant exposure.
  • Clause 7.2: Penetration test – Drop test with conical steel striker on shell crown after oven conditioning.
  • Clause 7.3: Flammability test – Direct burner flame exposure evaluating self-extinguishing duration and afterglow.
  • Clause 7.4: Electrical proof test – Immersion high-voltage insulation test to evaluate leakage current resistance.
  • Clause 7.5: Radiant heat test – Radiant thermal panel exposure test measuring interior temperature rise.

SPECIALIZED RESCUE STANDARD — ERGONOMIC DIVERGENCE
Emergency Service Allied Standard

IS 2745:1983 is designed for structural firefighting and rescue. It features heavy thermal composite layups and neck curtains that exceed industrial requirements while lacking the ventilated daily ergonomics and mass constraints of IS 2925:1984.
