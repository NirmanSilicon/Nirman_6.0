## 2. Verified System & Coverage Limitations (`OPEN` / `DEFERRED`)

1. **Narrow Document Type Coverage (`7` of `21` evaluated categories):**
   - Only `AADHAAR`, `INCOME_CERT`, `CASTE_CERT`, `RESIDENCE_CERT`, `MARKSHEET`, `BANK_PASSBOOK`, and `APPLICATION_FORM` exist in `DocumentType`.
   - **Unsupported:** Passport, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Ration Card (declared as a slot in `odisha.subhadra/pack.yaml` but unimplemented in `DocumentType`/`slot_classifier`/`extractor`), Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, Bank Statement (explicitly rejected), Utility Bills.
2. **Aadhaar & Marksheet Partial Support:**
   - `AADHAAR` does **not** extract `address`, parse QR codes, or verify UIDAI authenticity.
   - `MARKSHEET` does **not** distinguish Class 10 vs. Class 12 vs. University marksheets or extract degree/CGPA/SGPA.
3. **`DD <= 12` Ambiguous Date Downgrade (`parse_and_normalize_date`):**
   - Observed live: any `DD/MM/YYYY` date with `DD <= 12` (e.g., `10/08/2006` underage DOB or `10/02/2024` issue date) is tagged `EvidenceState.AMBIGUOUS`, causing `resolve_outcome()` to downgrade both `RULE-SUB-AGE-01` (underage defect) and `RULE-EXP-01` (valid cert) to `REVIEW` (`NEEDS_CHECKS`).
4. **Indic-Only Name Normalization (`normalize_name`):**
   - `unicodedata.combining()` in Stage 2 strips only Virama/Halant (`U+0B4D`/`U+094D`, `Mn`, `combining=9`), leaving `Lo` letters and `Mc` vowel signs (`U+0B3E`/`U+093E`) intact (`"ଅନନୟା ଦାସ"` / `"अननया दास"`). **Stage 5 (`re.sub(r'[^A-Z\s]', ' ', ...)` at `normalizer.py:74`)** then strips all non-ASCII Indic codepoints, returning `""`.
5. **Draft Status of `generic.demo`:**
   - `packs/generic.demo/pack.yaml` is `status: draft` and requires `allow_draft=True` (`--allow-draft`) to load.
6. **`MM/DD/YYYY` Rejection & Date Ambiguity Divergence:**
   - `parse_and_normalize_date("05/15/1990")` returns `INVALID_FORMAT` (`DATE_FORMATS` omits `%m/%d/%Y`).
   - `parse_and_normalize_date("2006-08-10")` returns `is_ambiguous=False` (correctly emitting `NOT_READY` under `odisha.subhadra`), whereas `get_date_ambiguity_candidates("2006-08-10")` inconsistently returns two candidates (`["2006-08-10", "2006-10-08"]`).
   - `safety_layer.py` `is_expiration_date_invariant_fail` is wired only to `issue_date` in legacy validation, not `age_on` / `dob` in `PackExecutionEngine`, so `10/08/2006` downgrades to `NEEDS_CHECKS` even though both `2006-08-10` and `2006-10-08` are underage.
7. **University Marksheet SGPA/CGPA/Grade Gap:**
   - `DocumentType.MARKSHEET` extracts `%` (`percentage`) only (`2/2` when `%` present); university marksheets with `SGPA`, `CGPA`, letter grades, total marks, or `PROVISIONAL GRADE SHEET` headers (`0/4`) are unsupported or rejected.
8. **Synthetic Corpus Scope & Small Denominators:**
   - Track A (`48` packets) uses simulated OCR parameters (`eval/harness.py`), and Track B native tests use synthetic PDFs (`n=1` to `n=7` per category, wide Wilson CIs); neither establishes real-world scanned citizen-document accuracy.
9. **ReportLab PDF Metadata Timestamp Variance (Comprehensive Suite):**
   - `comprehensive_eval_results.json`, `phase4_7_results.json`, and `phase9_results.json` embed `pdf_sha256` hashes of synthetic PDFs generated by ReportLab, which include `/CreationDate` and `/ID` metadata timestamps. Consequently, embedded PDF hashes differ between clean-checkout runs, while summary metrics (`7/7`, `9/9`, `34/34`), classifications, and rule outputs remain identical. (By contrast, B1 `eval/generators/generator_v2.py` produces byte-identical PDFs with `content_bundle_sha256: 7881ea3e...`.)
