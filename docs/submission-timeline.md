# NIRMAN 6.0 — HACKNATION 2026
# Submission Timeline

**Date:** 28–29 November 2026  
**All timings are in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30).**

All participating teams must follow the submission schedule below. Team leaders are responsible for ensuring that their team's latest work is pushed to their fork and made available to the organizers at each milestone.

## Submission Milestones

| Milestone | Date | Deadline | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Progress Submission | 28 November 2026 | **9:00 PM** | First push of the team's shared branch from the team leader's fork, just before the initial round of evaluation. |
| Second Progress Submission | 29 November 2026 | **3:00 AM** | Second push containing the team's updated implementation and progress. |
| Final Submission | 29 November 2026 | **6:00 AM** | Final push and submission of the completed project. No further changes should be considered part of the final submission unless permitted by the organizers. |

## Detailed Instructions

### 1. First Progress Submission — 9:00 PM, 28 November

- Team leaders must ensure that all completed work is pushed to their team's shared branch in their fork.
- The latest implementation must be available for the initial round of evaluation.
- Ensure that the corresponding pull request is created or updated against the assigned team branch in the original NIRMAN repository.
- This submission is intended to provide the organizers with an initial view of each team's progress.

### 2. Second Progress Submission — 3:00 AM, 29 November

- Teams can continue development after the first submission.
- Team leaders must push the latest changes to their shared branch in their fork.
- Update the corresponding pull request so that the organizers can access the latest implementation.
- Ensure that the major features and assigned tasks are properly integrated.

### 3. Final Submission — 6:00 AM, 29 November

- All development work intended for final evaluation must be completed.
- Team leaders must push the final version of the project to their shared branch.
- Ensure that the final pull request reflects the completed implementation and targets the correct team branch in the original repository.
- Verify that the repository contains all necessary source files and documentation.
- Check that no credentials, private information, or unnecessary datasets have been committed.

## Informing the Organizers

**Starting from 6:00 AM on 29 November 2026**, participants may inform the organizers about their final submission status.
Organizers can then verify the submitted work and proceed with the final evaluation process.
Participants will be allowed to go out of breakfast after final submission. 

## Important Reminders

- All deadlines are strict and follow IST.
- The team leader is responsible for coordinating and completing all three submissions.
- Ensure that the correct team branch is used throughout the process.
- All pull requests must target the corresponding team branch in the original NIRMAN repository, never `main`.
- Do not wait until the final deadline to resolve access issues, merge conflicts, or collaborator permissions.
- A successful push alone does not guarantee that the organizers have received or acknowledged the submission. Ensure the pull request is accessible.

**Plan your work accordingly and ensure that your team meets all three submission deadlines.**
