# Screenshots

Real captures from the running app, taken with Playwright and Chromium 153 (`deviceScaleFactor` 1 for desktop, the iPhone 12 profile for phone shots). Speech and vibration were stand-ins in the browser (so the haptic cards enable and the speech voices list shows two sample voices); everything else is the real page served by the real server. Two participants were really connected for the chat shots.
