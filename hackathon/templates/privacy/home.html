GOOD TO KNOW

Privacy, without the mystery.

Does MyPrivacyCheck access my accounts or device?

No. The assessment uses only the answers you choose to provide. It does not connect to your social accounts, inspect your device, or request social media passwords.

What does my score mean?

It is an educational, rule-based indicator based on self-reported answers. It is not a security audit and cannot guarantee complete privacy protection.

Can I improve my score?

Yes. Use the personalized actions to review your habits, then retake the assessment to compare your self-reported baseline.
