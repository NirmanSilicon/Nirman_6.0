PRIVACY TOOLKIT

Practical knowledge.
More confident choices.

Use these quick reminders as a starting point for your own privacy check-in.

01
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Make passwords unique

Use a different, strong password for each account. A reputable password manager can make that easier.

02
SIGN-IN PROTECTION

Turn on two-factor authentication

Start with your primary email, financial accounts, and social profiles. Prefer an authenticator app or security key where available.

03
APP PERMISSIONS

Review access periodically

Use your device's privacy settings to check camera, microphone, contacts, and location access. Remove access that no longer makes sense.

04
LOCATION

Share location intentionally

Prefer location access only while using an app, and pause public location tagging when you don't need it.

05
SOCIAL SHARING

Choose your audience

Check who can see your posts, profile details, and friend list. Remove old content that shares more than you intended.

06
CONNECTED SERVICES

Disconnect apps you no longer use

Review third-party services linked to important accounts and revoke access you no longer recognize or need.

Keep learning: Look for security and privacy guidance from official device makers, platform help centers, and government cybersecurity agencies. Availability and controls vary by service and region.
