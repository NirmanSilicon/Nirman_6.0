About the project

VIBRAFORCE is a cost-aware IoT solar panel tracker: a single-axis tracker built from low-cost parts and monitored online.

  1. SenseTwo LDRs measure light on the left and right of the panel.
  2. DecideThe ESP32 compares them and moves only when the gap is large enough.
  3. MoveThe SG90 servo turns the panel toward the brighter side.
  4. ReportReadings go to the OLED and to Blynk, and from there to this page.
