MODULE 01 · THE PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION (0:00 – 0:50)
[Press '1' on keyboard · Screen on Module 01]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE TARGET USERS:
Visually impaired and low-vision individuals performing daily tasks at a desk, 
dining table, or workstation.

THE CORE PROBLEM:
1. The White Cane stops at the floor: Canes navigate ground obstacles (0 to 15 cm) 
   for walking. At a table, when eating, typing, or working, the cane is useless.
2. Existing Vision Apps are a noisy firehose: Apps like Seeing AI or YOLO bark 
   10 objects a second into earphones ("Chair 80%, Table 90%, Laptop, Bottle..."). 
   Hearing is a blind person's primary sense. A noisy AI deafens them.
3. Zero memory: Standard models have no memory of what the user already knows, 
   and they get easily tricked by flat photos on screens.

HOW THE USER USES OUR PRODUCT:
An everyday Android smartphone worn in a simple chest harness or lanyard at chest 
level. It looks at the personal workspace hands-free.

OUR SOLUTION — SAARTHI:
A 100% offline personal-space cognitive companion. 
Unlike noisy object taggers, SAARTHI is PROACTIVELY SILENT. It speaks ONLY when 
physical reality changes from what the user believes.

How does this run on a normal smartphone without overheating or false alarms?

1. The Thermal Governor:
   Continuous 30fps camera + neural networks will overheat a phone in 10 minutes.
   We don't process every frame. We compare consecutive frame differences, process 
   only 4 to 5 key perception frames per second, and throttle hand tracking to 2 fps 
   when hands are away. The phone stays cool under 45°C.

2. Torso Oscillation Filtering:
   A phone worn at the chest sways as the user breathes or shifts in their chair.
   Standard magnetic compasses fail near laptops and steel desks. Our IMU 
   governor fuses gravity and gyroscope yaw, smoothing the "in front of you" 
   reference with a 10-second running average so left/right directions stay 
   dead stable.

3. 100% Offline Edge:
   Runs entirely on an everyday MediaTek Dimensity 700 phone. Zero cloud, zero API 
   costs, zero latency, complete personal privacy.
