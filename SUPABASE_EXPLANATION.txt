1. WHAT IS GOLDENHOUR?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GoldenHour is NOT an ambulance app and NOT a doctor app. 
GoldenHour owns the critical 12 to 15 minutes BEFORE the patient arrives at 
the Emergency Room (the "Golden Hour" of trauma and stroke survival).

Usually, hospitals have no preparation before an emergency patient arrives at 
their door. Doctors are in other wards, blood trays are not ready, and resuscitation 
beds are occupied.

With GoldenHour:
- As soon as a patient taps 4 simple questions on their phone, an emergency alert 
  is sent to the nearest capable hospital.
- 4 critical preparation tasks are instantly spawned:
  1. Reserve an ED Bed (Counter Team)
  2. Draw up life-saving emergency drugs (Medicine/Nurse Team)
  3. Calibrate ECG, Defibrillator & O2 (Equipment Team)
  4. Position a resuscitation stretcher at the entrance gate (Transport Team)
- A live countdown timer starts for every task.
- Hospital staff tap "Claim" on their phones, and the hospital ops wall screen 
  updates instantly.
- When the patient arrives, the medical team is standing at the gate, ready.

2. WHY SUPABASE IS THE PERFECT FIT FOR THIS HACKATHON
================================================================================
In a hackathon with strict time limits, building a custom Node.js/Python backend 
with WebSocket servers, Redis caches, background worker queues, and custom auth 
would take 48+ hours and still be fragile.

Supabase gives us an enterprise-grade backend in a single platform:
1. Zero custom backend servers needed: The database IS our backend engine.
2. Built-in WebSockets (Realtime): We didn't have to write a single line of Socket.io.
3. Sub-2-second sync: When a nurse taps "Claim" in a ward, the emergency wall screen 
   flashes green across the hospital in less than 2 seconds.
4. Military-grade security built-in: Row Level Security (RLS) ensures Hospital A 
   cannot see Hospital B's patients, and nurses cannot read private doctor notes.
5. Zero race conditions: Everything is solved right inside PostgreSQL using 
   atomic transactions.

3. CORE SUPABASE FEATURES USED IN GOLDENHOUR (SIMPLE BREAKDOWN)
================================================================================

A. SUPABASE REALTIME (WebSockets)
- What it does: Whenever an alert or task is created or updated in the database, 
  Supabase automatically pushes that change to every open phone and dashboard over 
  WebSockets.
- Why it matters for GoldenHour: In emergencies, doctors cannot sit and refresh 
  a browser page. When an alert arrives, the hospital screen immediately sounds 
  a chime and displays the new card without page reloading.
- Realtime Tables: alerts, alert_tasks, bed_holds, notification_outbox.

B. DATABASE STORED PROCEDURES / RPCs ("The Engine in SQL")
- Instead of writing business logic in standard API routes that can crash or lag, 
  our entire state machine runs inside Supabase PostgreSQL as stored functions:
  * claim_task(task_id):
    Atomically assigns a task to the logged-in staff member. If two nurses tap 
    "Claim" at the exact same millisecond, the database gives it to the first one 
    and immediately informs the second who claimed it. Nobody is assigned twice!
  * next_state(task_id, action):
    Transitions tasks (OPEN -> CLAIMED -> DONE) and advances the emergency alert.
  * escalate_overdue():
    Checks if any task is taking longer than its SLA (e.g., 90s or 120s). If so, 
    it immediately flags it as ESCALATED and triggers an alarm for the supervisor.
  * engine_tick():
    The heartbeat function. Runs every 10-30 seconds, checks all overdue timers, 
    expires stale alerts, and safely returns unused beds. Protected by an 
    Advisory Lock so two tickers never conflict.
  * cancel_alert(alert_id):
    If the family diverts to another hospital, one tap cancels the alert, 
    immediately frees the held emergency bed, and records the reason in the ledger.

C. ROW LEVEL SECURITY (RLS) & PRIVACY
- In healthcare, patient privacy (HIPAA / GDPR) is non-negotiable.
- Supabase RLS enforces rules at the database level:
  * Hospital Isolation: Staff at Capital Hospital can NEVER see alerts belonging 
    to AIIMS Bhubaneswar.
  * Role Separation: Only emergency doctors can read full clinical diagnosis 
    notes. Nurses receive a sanitized "nurse_safe_view" (medicines, allergies, 
    watch-for items) without private doctor notes.
  * Anti-Tampering: Regular users cannot bypass the system by writing direct 
    UPDATE queries to change task states. All changes MUST go through the 
    validated engine RPCs.

D. EPHEMERAL BED HOLDS (Anti-Double Booking)
- When an emergency alert fires, 1 hospital bed is reserved for 30 minutes.
- We built a partial unique index:
  CREATE UNIQUE INDEX uq_active_bed_hold ON bed_holds(facility_id, bed_label) 
  WHERE released_at IS NULL;
- This makes it physically impossible for two different patients to be assigned 
  the same bed at the same time. If an alert is cancelled or arrives, the hold is 
  cleanly released.

E. TYPE-SAFE CLIENT INTEGRATION
- Supabase allows exporting TypeScript types directly matching our PostgreSQL 
  tables (Database['public']['Tables']).
- Front-end developers have 100% autocomplete and compile-time type safety 
  with zero guesswork.

4. REAL-LIFE 12-MINUTE SCENARIO (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================
Minute 00:00 - Emergency Triggered
- A patient experiencing chest pain submits 4 taps on the GoldenHour web app.
- Supabase automatically inserts a row into 'alerts'.
- A PostgreSQL database trigger instantly spawns 4 preparation tasks into 
  'alert_tasks' with countdown deadlines (90s, 120s, 180s) and decrements 
  the hospital's free bed count.
- Supabase Realtime notifies the hospital intake screen with an audio chime.

Minute 00:30 - Simultaneous Claim
- Two nurses see the "Prepare cardiac medications" task.
- Both tap "Claim" at the same second.
- Supabase's 'claim_task' executes an atomic compare-and-set:
  Nurse Arati wins the claim. Nurse Priyanka gets an instant polite notification: 
  "Already claimed by Sister Arati Jena".
- Task card turns from Gray (Open) to Blue (Claimed).

Minute 02:00 - Overdue Escalation (The Clock Never Sleeps)
- Stretcher team hasn't claimed their task after 90 seconds.
- Background 'engine_tick' detects the breach, moves state to 'ESCALATED', 
  and broadcasts an urgent AMBER flash to the supervisor wall screen.
- Duty supervisor immediately delegates transport staff.

Minute 08:00 - Pre-Arrival Completion
- All 4 tasks reach 'DONE'.
- Alert automatically moves to 'ALL_CLAIMED'.
- Resuscitation bed is locked, IV medications drawn, ECG calibrated, stretcher 
  waiting at gate.

Minute 12:00 - Patient Crosses Gate
- Front desk searches 6-character code and taps "Arrived".
- Escalation clock freezes. Patient receives zero-delay emergency care!

5. HACKATHON PITCH CHEAT SHEET (WHAT TO TELL JUDGES)
================================================================================
Q: "Why did you choose Supabase instead of building a Node/Express backend?"
A: "In emergency healthcare, every millisecond counts. By using Supabase, we 
    eliminated entire network hops. Our business logic, concurrency control, and 
    audit logging execute directly inside PostgreSQL through atomic RPCs. 
    Plus, Supabase Realtime gives us sub-2-second WebSocket sync between mobile 
    phones and hospital wall dashboards with zero infrastructure setup."

Q: "How do you handle race conditions when multiple staff claim the same task?"
A: "We handle this at the database level using atomic conditional updates inside 
    a SECURITY DEFINER stored procedure. The first transaction locks the row; 
    the second transaction cleanly fails and receives the winner's identity. 
    Nobody is ever dispatched twice."

Q: "How is patient data kept secure?"
A: "We use Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) with hospital isolation functions. 
    Staff can only access data belonging to their own facility, and clinical 
    records are segregated by role—nurses read a sanitized view while doctors 
    maintain diagnosis confidentiality."

Q: "What makes this production-grade rather than a hackathon prototype?"
A: "Our database includes advisory transaction locks, automated audit logging 
    in an append-only events ledger, partial unique indexes for bed reservations, 
    and fully synchronized TypeScript definitions across our Next.js frontend."
